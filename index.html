The Journey Ahead

Building the future of on-chain gaming, one phase at a time

Phase 1: Foundation ✅
Q4 2024 - COMPLETE
Core infrastructure deployed. Chess & Checkers live with full on-chain verification, staking rewards, and NFT achievements.
Phase 2: Multi-Token 🔄
Q1 2025 - READY
Enable players to stake with any token (USDT, USDC, etc.). Automatic swapping to CORE via decentralized exchange integration.
Phase 3: Cross-Chain 🌉
Q1 2025 - READY
Players from different blockchains can compete. LayerZero integration enables seamless cross-chain gaming experiences.
Phase 4: DAO Governance 🏛️
Q2 2025 - READY
Community-driven platform decisions. REALM token holders vote on new features, games, and economic parameters.
Phase 5: Combat Arena 🥊
Q2 2025
Launch of fighting game with Mortal Kombat-style gameplay. Multiple characters, special moves, and tournament brackets.
Phase 6: Racing & Pool 🏎️
Q3-Q4 2025
High-speed racing and precision pool games. Physics engines, upgradable vehicles, and competitive ranked play.
Phase 7: VR MetaRealm 🥽
2026
Full VR integration with 3D immersive worlds. Support for major VR headsets, full body tracking, and social interactions—all on-chain.
